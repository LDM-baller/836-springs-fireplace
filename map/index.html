Where this map is soft

The survey shows one merged footprint. It labels 2 Story Frame Dwelling, Porch, Brick Patio and Wood Steps — it does not distinguish the barn, and it does not show a separate outbuilding. So the barn and shed-range markers are placed from what the photographs show, not from the drawing.

The plan is dated during this renovation, not before it. Everything marked Proposed — the deck, the driveway, the septic — is the current work; everything marked Existing is what the previous renovation left behind. So this single drawing is both the old footprint and the new one, depending on which labels you read.

The pool is not on this plan at all, though it is in the album photographs twice.
